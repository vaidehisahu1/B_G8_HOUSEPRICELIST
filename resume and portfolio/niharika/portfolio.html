US House Price Analysis Across 10 States
Group · DVA Capstone

End-to-end Python ETL pipeline and Tableau dashboard suite analysing 11,410 residential listings across 10 US states, uncovering cross-state pricing patterns for investors and brokerage firms.

  • Built a 5-notebook ETL pipeline with feature engineering: price_per_sqft, weekday, is_weekend

  • Scenario analysis: removing California collapses national avg by 45% ($975K → $534K)
